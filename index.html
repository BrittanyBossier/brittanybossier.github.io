About Me

I began exploring herbalism after being diagnosed with Hashimoto’s thyroiditis. I was immediately drawn to the holistic approach of herbalism and found that not only did herbs help improve my physical well-being, but the intention and love that go into the practice of herbalism did wonders for my mental and emotional health as well. As an herbalist, I aim to make others feel seen and heard so they can feel supported in their healing journey.

I love traveling, reading, and spending time outside with my husband and dog. I have worked in clinical research for 14 years, I’m a certified yoga instructor and have completed my 3 years of Clinical Herbalist training at Gathering Thyme School of Western Herbalism. I look forward to sharing my love of herbs with you!

Whether you're brand new to herbs or looking to deepen an existing practice, I'm here to guide you.
